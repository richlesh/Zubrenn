Zubrenn: First Claim

By the year 2500, Earth had finally learned to act as one world. Nations that had once fought over borders now shared a single ambition: to reach the stars. The United Earth Expedition set out to find a new home—and found one closer than anyone had dared hope.

Orbiting Alpha Centauri was Zubrenn, a world of oceans, forests, and broad continents. Its air was breathable. Its water was clean. Its living landscape was unlike anything humanity had ever seen. For Earth, Zubrenn was more than a discovery. It was a second chance.

Then came the transmissions.

Humanity was not the first to chart the planet. Across the galaxy, other civilizations had detected Zubrenn too. Some were already sending ships. All wanted a foothold on this rare, habitable world—and none intended to yield it.

Earth’s leaders feared what an alien power established on Zubrenn might mean for the future of humanity. So they launched a colony ship with a simple mission: arrive, survive, and secure as much of the planet as possible.

You lead that first human settlement. The world is vast, and each turn brings new choices: where to build, what to explore, and how to grow across Zubrenn’s vast lands. Every new settlement strengthens Earth’s claim—but rival civilizations are advancing, and the planet may not remain open for long.

Build a civilization worthy of the stars. Claim Zubrenn before someone else does.
