## Bolthara

### Description:

![Bolthara](../../aliens/Bolthara.png)

The Bolthara are a species forged by crushing gravity, and it shows in every line of their immense, slab-muscled frames. Their skeletons are reinforced with dense, metallic bone that rings faintly when struck, and their broad bodies are built low and powerful, with thick limbs that could bend iron bars without strain. Raised on worlds where a careless fall could prove fatal, they move with surprising deliberateness — a Bolthara wastes no motion, for on their homeworld every motion costs dearly.

Endurance, more than raw power, lies at the heart of what the Bolthara admire. Their most sacred gatherings are the great **communal feasts**, days-long celebrations where the entire community eats, labors, tells stories, and bears burdens together not to display dominance but to prove that they can endure. The one who remains standing, generous, and good-humored longest earns the highest honor — not the strongest arm, but the most unbreakable spirit. These feasts are where bonds are forged, debts forgiven, and the young taught that strength is meaningless unless it serves the gathered many.

In the wider galaxy, where gravity is gentle and the air is thin by Bolthara reckoning, their strength becomes almost mythic. They lift and haul and build with ease, and many take pride in the heaviest, hardest labor other species cannot manage. Yet they are not brutes; their culture reveres patience, loyalty, and the quiet steadiness of those who carry weight — literal and otherwise — without complaint. A Bolthara's word, once given, is said to be as unyielding as their bones.

### Notable Traits:

- Habitat: High-gravity worlds and heavy-industry colonies
- Lifespan: 110–140 years
- Strength: Immense physical power and remarkable endurance
- Weakness: Slow, ponderous, and uneasy in low gravity or confined spaces
- Temperament: Steadfast, hearty, and generous

### Fun Fact:

A Bolthara feast is scored not by who eats the most, but by who is still cheerfully serving others when the last dish is cleared.
