## Ithkane

### Description:

![Ithkane](../../aliens/Ithkane.png)

The Ithkane are a graceful, long-necked people of the deep deserts, their slender frames built for the endless horizon. Their most arresting feature is a pair of mirrored eyes — smooth, reflective orbs that throw back the glare of twin suns and let them gaze unblinking across the shimmering dunes. Their skin is a dusty ochre that pales or darkens with the seasons, and loose, layered robes protect them from blowing sand while catching the faintest breath of wind to cool them.

The Ithkane do not build cities so much as remember routes. Their civilization moves in great singing caravans that trace ancient paths between the hidden waters of the waste, and their songs are both art and atlas — long, winding melodies in which the location of every spring, every buried aquifer, and every safe campsite is encoded. A caravan master is first and foremost a singer, and the loss of a song is mourned as the loss of a whole province of the world. Children learn to walk and to sing the water-lines in the same season.

Their uncanny ability to detect water beneath dry ground is the center of their faith and their art. The Ithkane believe that water is the hidden blood of the world, and that it calls to those who learn to listen; their dowsers, called Deepsingers, enter a trance and hum until, they say, the water hums back. To find a new spring is the most sacred of achievements, celebrated with days of song and the founding of a new verse in the caravan's eternal melody — and where the water is found the Ithkane dig and line wells, raise cisterns, and cut simple channels and waystones, leaving a thread of modest waterworks along the routes they travel. Hospitality at a shared well is absolute, and even blood-feuds are suspended at water, for the Ithkane hold that to deny a thirsty soul is to deny the world itself.

### Notable Traits:

- Habitat: Vast deserts and dune seas
- Lifespan: 110 years
- Strength: Can sense groundwater and navigate trackless waste
- Weakness: Few in number and vulnerable when settled in one place
- Temperament: Melodic, hospitable, and proud

### Fun Fact:

An Ithkane can walk for days guided by nothing but a half-remembered song fragment, and will stop mid-journey to compose a new verse the instant a fresh spring is found.
