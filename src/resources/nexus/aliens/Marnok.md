## Marnok

### Description:

![Marnok](../../aliens/Marnok.png)

The Marnok are short, immensely sturdy beings whose skin has the gray, pitted texture of weathered granite and nearly the hardness besides. Squat and powerful, built low and wide like the boulders among which they dwell, they can withstand pressures and impacts that would pulverize softer creatures, and the crushing depths of the deep mines and ocean trenches hold no terror for them. Their broad, blunt-fingered hands are made for shaping stone, and their deep-set eyes regard the world with the unhurried steadiness of a people who think in geological time.

Patience is the supreme Marnok virtue. They are craftspeople to the marrow, and their crafts are the slow, enduring kind — stonework, metalcraft, the carving of gems, the raising of structures meant to stand for ten thousand years. A Marnok does not rush, cannot be rushed, and regards haste as the mother of all shoddy work. Their masterpieces may take a lifetime to complete, passed from a master's hands to an apprentice's and finished by a third generation, and this is not seen as a burden but as the natural way of things, for the Marnok measure worth by endurance and nothing endures that is made in a hurry.

Their stone-hard skin and resistance to pressure let them labor in places no other species can reach — the deepest shafts, the hearts of mountains, the crushing dark beneath the sea — and so they have become the galaxy's unmatched miners and deep-builders, bringing up ores and gems and raising foundations in conditions that would kill others outright. This capacity shapes a culture that is grounded, literally and figuratively: the Marnok trust what is solid, distrust what is quick and glittering, and judge all things by whether they will last.

Marnok society is organized into ancient craft-houses, each guarding its techniques and traditions with jealous pride, and governed by elder masters whose authority rests on the sheer quality of what they have made. Taciturn and slow to warm, the Marnok are nonetheless the most dependable of allies and the most honest of dealers; a Marnok's work is their word, and both are made to last. They speak little, promise less, and deliver always.

### Notable Traits:

- Habitat: Deep mines, mountain roots, and high-pressure depths
- Lifespan: 160 years
- Strength: Stone-hard skin and the ability to endure crushing pressure
- Weakness: Slow-moving and inflexible, uncomfortable with sudden change
- Temperament: Patient, taciturn, and reliable

### Fun Fact:

Some Marnok masterworks take three generations to complete, and a craftsman considers it a point of honor to begin a project they know they will never live to see finished.
