## Prellun

### Description:

![Prellun](../../aliens/Prellun.png)

The Prellun are small, smooth-skinned beings, delicate and quick, with sleek hairless hides and enormous dark eyes that dominate their slender faces like twin pools of liquid night. Those eyes are the first thing anyone notices and the last thing anyone forgets, forever wide, forever drinking in the world with an expression of frank and inexhaustible wonder. The Prellun are the galaxy's great questioners, a people who seem physically incapable of leaving a stone unturned, a door unopened, or a mystery unpoked, and who regard incuriosity as a kind of living death.

Exploration is not merely their occupation but their creed. A young Prellun's rite of passage is to venture alone into the unknown and return with something no one in the community has seen before — a strange rock, a new river, a tale of a far horizon — and the greatest honor their society bestows is reserved for those who have mapped the most, charted the farthest, and asked the questions no one else thought to ask. Their settlements are cheerful, cluttered places, part museum and part workshop, overflowing with the trophies of a thousand expeditions and buzzing with the ceaseless chatter of a people who would always rather find out than be told. They are poor keepers of secrets and worse keepers of still, for a Prellun at rest is a Prellun already itching to be gone.

Their most extraordinary gift lies behind those great eyes: the Prellun perceive rapid motion with astonishing clarity, their vision running so quick that the world seems, to them, to move in a gentle slow-motion. A dart that another would never see coming hangs lazily in a Prellun's sight; the blur of a hummingbird's wing resolves into separate, deliberate strokes; a fall, a strike, a sudden danger all unfold with time to spare. This quickness of sight makes them peerless scouts and nimble dodgers, forever a half-heartbeat ahead of catastrophe, and it has stamped itself upon their restless temperament. The Prellun see so much, so fast, that stillness bores them to distraction; the world to them is a flickering wealth of detail that others are simply too slow to notice, and they cannot understand why anyone would willingly look away.

### Notable Traits:

- Habitat: Everywhere and anywhere — frontier camps, strange coasts, unmapped wilds
- Lifespan: 70–95 years, spent in constant motion
- Strength: Perceive fast motion with uncanny clarity; brilliant scouts and dodgers
- Weakness: Physically slight; easily bored and prone to reckless wandering
- Temperament: Inquisitive, restless, and irrepressibly curious

### Fun Fact:

A Prellun can catch a thrown object out of the air so casually that larger peoples sometimes test them for sport — and have never once won.
