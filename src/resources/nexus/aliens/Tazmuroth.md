## Tazmuroth

### Description:

![Tazmuroth](../../aliens/Tazmuroth.png)

The Tazmuroth are large, imposing beings whose heavy frames are crowned by curving tusks and whose broad faces bear distinctive **heat-sensing pits** between brow and snout. Through these pits they perceive the warmth of living things and the glow of hidden fires, reading the world in a layer of heat invisible to most. Despite their formidable bulk and fearsome tusks, they move with a slow dignity, and their deep, resonant voices carry the measured cadence of a people who weigh their words.

For all their physical power, the Tazmuroth are a civilization built not on force but on **law and debate**. Their revered **lawkeepers** settle every dispute — from petty quarrels to matters of war — through formal, ritualized argument conducted before the gathered community. To raise a hand in anger where words would serve is, to the Tazmuroth, the deepest shame, a confession that one's reasoning has failed. Their courts are grand halls of oratory where champions of each side marshal evidence, precedent, and eloquence, and victory goes to the better argument rather than the stronger arm.

This devotion to reasoned discourse has made the Tazmuroth patient, principled, and deeply respectful of precedent and procedure. Their children are schooled in rhetoric and the recitation of law from an early age, and the most honored elders are not the mightiest but the most persuasive and fair-minded. Their heat-sense lends a quiet depth to their judgment — they are famously hard to deceive, for a lie, they say, carries a flush of its own — and they take pride in a justice that is slow, deliberate, and cooled always by the discipline of debate.

### Notable Traits:

- Habitat: Warm lowlands, river valleys, and grand hall-cities
- Lifespan: 130–160 years
- Strength: Heat-sensing perception, imposing presence, and formidable rhetorical skill
- Weakness: Slow to act and bound by lengthy procedure
- Temperament: Dignified, principled, and deliberate

### Fun Fact:

A Tazmuroth lawkeeper claims to detect a lie by the sudden warmth of a speaker's face, making their courts notoriously difficult places to deceive.
