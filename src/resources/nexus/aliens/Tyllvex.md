## Tyllvex

### Description:

![Tyllvex](../../aliens/Tyllvex.png)

The Tyllvex are tiny, quicksilver beings, each no larger than a human thumb, their slight bodies kept aloft by a blur of translucent wings that catch the light in flickers of rainbow. They dart and hover with astonishing speed, their movements almost too fast for larger eyes to follow, and they communicate in rapid, high-pitched trills that the slower peoples can scarcely parse. For all their smallness, they are a numerous and industrious folk, and where one Tyllvex is found, a thousand of its kin are surely near.

Their homes are marvels of miniature craft: delicate dwellings woven and carved within the hollows of great trees, intricate warrens of chambers, galleries, and spiraling passages adorned with filigree finer than frost. Entire Tyllvex cities nest within a single ancient trunk, and the loss of such a tree is a civilizational calamity. They are devoted woodwrights and weavers, and their artistry — invisible to the careless and only truly appreciated under magnification — is a source of fierce communal pride.

The Tyllvex possess an exquisite sensitivity to changes in air pressure, feeling the subtlest shift of the coming weather in the fine hairs along their wings. This gift makes them peerless forecasters, and their society is ordered around the reading of the sky's moods: the Pressure-Singers trill warnings of coming storms, and whole communities move, shelter, or celebrate at their word. Their faith holds that the air itself is alive — a vast, breathing presence they call the Great Breath — and that to feel its changes is to feel the pulse of a living god. Cheerful, swift, and endlessly busy, the Tyllvex live brief, bright lives attuned to every tremor of the wind.

### Notable Traits:

- Habitat: Hollow trees and ancient forest canopies
- Lifespan: 20 years
- Strength: Senses air-pressure changes; swift, agile flight
- Weakness: Tiny and fragile; devastated by the loss of a home-tree
- Temperament: Energetic, communal, and cheerful

### Fun Fact:

Tyllvex throw an impromptu festival the moment their Pressure-Singers announce fair weather, and an equally elaborate one to brace for a storm — any excuse, really.
