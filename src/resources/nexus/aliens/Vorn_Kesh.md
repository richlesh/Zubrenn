## Vorn Kesh

### Description:

![Vorn Kesh](../../aliens/Vorn_Kesh.png)

The Vorn Kesh are a broad, powerfully built people whose bodies are armored in overlapping plates of mineral-dense scale, giving them the look of walking boulders worn smooth by a hundred desert winds. Their limbs are thick and tireless, capable of hauling an entire camp's worth of goods across open country without complaint. Beneath that stony hide runs a slow and ancient warmth, for Vorn Kesh skin drinks in the heat of the day and releases it gently through the long cold nights — a living furnace that keeps both the bearer and anyone sheltered beside them alive on the harshest of worlds.

They are nomads by blood and by philosophy, following seasonal routes their ancestors mapped across generations. A Vorn Kesh clan carries no permanent home; home is the circle of kin and the fire they gather around. Among them, hospitality is not merely a courtesy but the highest law. To turn away a traveler, even an enemy, is considered a crime against the clan's honor, and the greatest insult a Vorn Kesh can offer is to let a guest leave hungry. Their long migrations are punctuated by great gatherings where rival clans set aside grudges to share salt, stone-bread, and the warmth of their own bodies through the freezing dark.

Their beliefs center on the idea of the Everhearth — a cosmic warmth they believe flows through all living things and binds strangers into kin. Vorn Kesh elders, called Hearthmothers and Hearthfathers, press their palms to a newcomer's shoulder and share body heat as a sacred greeting, a ritual that doubles as both blessing and the practical gift of survival. Their society is loosely egalitarian, organized around reputation earned through generosity; the wealthiest Vorn Kesh is not the one with the most goods but the one who has given the most away. In this way their heat-sharing bodies have shaped a culture where warmth, literal and figurative, is the only currency that truly matters.

### Notable Traits:

- Habitat: Arid plains and cold deserts
- Lifespan: 150 years
- Strength: Immense physical power and body-heat regulation
- Weakness: Slow-moving and reluctant to abandon kin
- Temperament: Gruff but deeply generous

### Fun Fact:

A Vorn Kesh who has gone a full day without offering warmth to another is said to have "a cold shoulder," the origin of their harshest insult.
