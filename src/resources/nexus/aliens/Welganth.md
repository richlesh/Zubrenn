## Welganth

### Description:

![Welganth](../../aliens/Welganth.png)

The Welganth are gentle, slow-moving nomads whose broad, domed shells rise from their backs like living hills. Within and upon those shells they carry something unique among wandering peoples: **household gardens** — soil-filled terraces where herbs, mosses, and small crops grow, tended lovingly as the Welganth journey. Their thick, placid limbs and patient gait set the unhurried pace of a people who carry their whole world upon their backs, and their shells, patterned with the greens of what they cultivate, are gardens and homes at once.

To the Welganth, **home is not a place but a gathering**. Having no fixed settlements, they define belonging entirely through the company they keep, and a Welganth caravan considers itself fully at home wherever it halts to share a meal and tend its traveling gardens together. This conviction shapes a culture of radical hospitality and portability: possessions are few and precious, community is everything, and the warmest word in their language means something close to "wherever we are, together." A lone Welganth is thought not homeless but merely between homes, awaiting the next gathering.

Their shell-gardens are more than sustenance; they are heirlooms and histories. Cuttings are passed between families at gatherings, so that a single sprig of herb might travel generations and continents, binding distant caravans through shared living roots. The Welganth measure wealth in the diversity and health of what they grow, and their customs revolve around the sowing, sharing, and savoring of the garden-born feast. Patient, kind, and endlessly welcoming, they drift across the land carrying green life wherever they go, convinced that any patch of ground becomes home the moment friends sit down to eat upon it.

### Notable Traits:

- Habitat: Nomadic; migratory routes across temperate lands
- Lifespan: 160–200 years
- Strength: Self-sufficient portable gardens and deep communal bonds
- Weakness: Slow and burdened; vulnerable when scattered or alone
- Temperament: Gentle, hospitable, and patient

### Fun Fact:

A single herb cutting may travel for generations across a Welganth caravan's shell-gardens, so that families on opposite ends of a continent still share the same living plant.
