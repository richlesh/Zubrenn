## Yssibel

### Description:

![Yssibel](../../aliens/Yssibel.png)

The Yssibel are a small, nimble, foxlike people, lithe of frame and quick of movement, with sharp triangular ears that swivel independently atop their heads and long, bushy tails ringed in alternating bands of russet and cream. Those ears are their pride and their power: a Yssibel can catch the whisper of a footstep three rooms away, the tremor of a half-truth in a trading partner's voice, or the distant rustle of weather long before it arrives. In a species that hears everything, nothing of importance ever goes unheard.

Yssibel society runs on negotiation. Where other peoples might settle matters by force or by rigid law, the Yssibel settle them by conversation — endless, elaborate, delicious conversation, in which every party talks, listens, offers, and counter-offers until a bargain pleasing to all is struck. A skilled negotiator is their equivalent of a hero, celebrated in song and story, and children play elaborate bargaining games almost before they can walk. To be called "silver-tongued" among the Yssibel is the finest of compliments; to be called "deaf to the room" is a grave insult indeed.

Their extraordinary hearing shapes everything about how they bargain. A Yssibel listens not merely to words but to the breath between them, the quickening pulse of excitement, the faint hesitation that betrays a bluff. This gives them an almost unfair advantage in any dealing, and they know it — which is why their culture binds their cleverness with strict codes of fair play. A bargain struck in bad faith is dishonored forever, and a Yssibel who exploits their gift to cheat the honest is cast out of the trading-circles that are the lifeblood of their world.

They live in sprawling, warren-like communities full of winding passages and cozy burrows, organized loosely around trading houses that compete and cooperate in equal measure. The Yssibel are curious, playful, and irrepressibly social; they delight in riddles, gossip, and the thrust-and-parry of a good debate. Beneath the levity, though, runs a shrewd intelligence that few other species fully appreciate until they find themselves having agreed to far more than they intended.

### Notable Traits:

- Habitat: Forest-edge warrens and bustling trade-towns
- Lifespan: 70 years
- Strength: Exceptional hearing and masterful, persuasive negotiation
- Weakness: Physically slight and easily overwhelmed in loud, chaotic environments
- Temperament: Clever, curious, and sociable

### Fun Fact:

Yssibel courts settle disputes through "listening duels," in which the judge rules in favor of whoever first detects a lie in the opponent's testimony.
