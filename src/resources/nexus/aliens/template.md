## Vessari

### Description:

<span class="float-right">![road](../../aliens/Vessari.png)</span>

The Vessari are a highly intelligent aquatic species originating from the deep oceanic world of Thalassa Prime. Resembling the humble goldfish of Earth, these unassuming creatures hide one of the galaxy's most brilliant engineering minds behind their perpetually unimpressed expressions.

Having evolved in a world entirely covered by water, the Vessari faced a unique challenge: how does a species with no limbs, no lungs, and no tolerance for dry land ever reach the stars? Their answer was the Ambulatory Exploration Suit (AES) — a rugged, humanoid exosuit topped with a reinforced aqua-dome helmet that serves as a portable biosphere. The suit responds to subtle fin movements and bioelectric signals, allowing a Vessari to walk, grip, and build with surprising dexterity.

### Notable Traits:

- Habitat: Ocean
- Lifespan: 200+ years (they claim the "3-second memory" rumor is slanderous propaganda)
- Strength: Master engineers and patient problem-solvers
- Weakness: A cracked dome is a very bad day
- Temperament: Famously grumpy-looking, but generally diplomatic

### Fun Fact:

Vessari explorers never travel alone — each suit carries a backup water reserve affectionately called "the spare bowl."
